# Model Improvement Ideas

This document captures potential improvements to the PlayeRank extended framework for future development.

> **Status key:** ✅ Implemented · 🔄 Partially implemented · ⬜ Not yet started

---

## Feature Engineering

- ⬜ **Game context features**: Encode match state (score at time of action, minute of the match, home/away) as additional input features. A pass made while 1-0 up in the 85th minute carries different value than the same pass at 0-0 in the 10th minute.
- ⬜ **Opponent strength adjustment**: Weight player scores by the quality of the opponent. Scoring against a top team should be valued more than scoring against the weakest side.
- ✅ **Sequence/chain context**: Model multi-event chains (e.g. pass → dribble → shot) rather than isolated events. The contribution of each action in a chain leading to a goal is currently invisible to the model.

  **Implementation status:**

  1. ✅ **Chain detection** — `chainFeatures._detect_chains()` sorts events by `(matchPeriod, eventSec)` and breaks chains on `INTERRUPTION`, `FOUL`, `OFFSIDE`, period change, or `teamId` change. Events with `playerId=0` or `teamId=0` are excluded.

  2. ✅ **Chain outcome classification** — `chainFeatures._classify_chain()` returns `'goal'`, `'shot'`, or `None` based on the terminal event and `GOAL_TAG`.

  3. ✅ **Feature class `chainFeatures.py`** — Extends `Feature`. Produces eight features per player/team per match:
     - Positive: `chain-shot-participant`, `chain-goal-participant`, `chain-final-action`
     - Harmful: `chain-turnover-precedes-shot`, `chain-turnover-precedes-goal`, `chain-turnover-final-actor`, `chain-conceded-shot`, `chain-conceded-goal`

  4. ✅ **Pipeline integration** — Chain features are included in `compute_features_weight.py` (positive chain features only, via `featureFilters`) and `compute_lack_of_performance.py` (all chain features, split by filter).

  5. ✅ **README updates** — Chain context model section added covering chain detection rules, positive features, harmful features, `chainScore`, `chainWasteScore`, and `chainNetScore`.

  6. ✅ **Dashboard updates** — `⛓️ Chain Analytics` tab added with chain score scatter, chain participation leaderboard and scatter, harmful chain leaderboard and scatter, and chain origin pitch heatmap. Player Profile tab extended with chain participation timeline.

  7. ✅ **Data volume** — ~3.25 M events across 1,941 matches confirmed. `playerId=0` events (226,038 — 7% of data) filtered at source in `chainFeatures.py` and at load time in the dashboard.

  8. ✅ **Chain pitch visualisation** — Chain origin heatmap implemented in the Chain Analytics tab using `go.Histogram2dContour` on the existing `draw_soccer_field()` figure. `chain_map.json` generated by `compute_lack_of_performance.py`. Single chain replay, player contribution map, and chain length vs outcome views remain ⬜ not yet started.

- ✅ **Minutes-played normalisation**: Divide per-match scores by actual minutes played rather than treating a 90-minute appearance the same as a 10-minute substitute appearance.

  **Implementation status:**

  1. ✅ **Pipeline** — `compute_lack_of_performance.py` computes `*_per90` variants for all six score columns (`playerankScore`, `wasteScore`, `netScore`, `chainScore`, `chainWasteScore`, `chainNetScore`) immediately after net score derivation. Uses `clip(lower=1)` to guard against zero-minute edge cases from `fillna(0)`. Scores are `raw / (minutesPlayed / 90)`.

  2. ✅ **Dashboard toggle** — Sidebar `Score type` radio (`Per 90 min` | `Raw`, default: Per 90 min) switches all leaderboards, summary metrics, and player profile charts between `_per90` and raw columns. Falls back gracefully to raw-only when the CSV predates the change. Header caption in the Leaderboard tab confirms when per-90 mode is active.

## Model Architecture

- ⬜ **Role-specific models**: Train separate SVM models for each role cluster rather than a single global model. Defensive actions are undervalued by a model trained on all positions simultaneously.
- ⬜ **Non-linear model**: Replace LinearSVC with a gradient-boosted tree or neural network to capture interaction effects between features (e.g. a high shot rate matters more when combined with high positioning quality).
- ⬜ **Uncertainty quantification**: Report a confidence interval around each player score, not just a point estimate. Players with few appearances should have wider intervals to prevent small-sample flukes from dominating leaderboards.

---

## Label Quality

- ⬜ **xG differential instead of match result**: Replace binary win/loss labels with the match-level expected goals differential. This provides a continuous, less noisy training signal and decouples luck (post shots, goalkeeper errors) from genuine team performance.
- ⬜ **Weighted match outcomes**: Down-weight heavily lopsided results (e.g. 5-0 wins) when training, since they may reflect opponent weakness more than individual quality.

---

## Aggregation and Ranking

- ⬜ **Time-decay weighting**: Apply an exponential decay so that recent matches contribute more to the current rating than matches from early in the season. Useful for tracking form.
- ⬜ **Plus-minus component**: Add a simple on/off plus-minus term (team goal difference when the player is on the field vs. off) to complement the action-based playerankScore.

## Waste Model

- ✅ **Independent feature sets**: `playerank/features/featureFilters.py` partitions quality features into non-overlapping performance and waste subsets:
  - **Performance Weighter** trains only on positive-outcome features (`accurate`, `assist`, `key pass`, `opportunity`, `counter attack`, `Free Kick-Penalty`) — 34 features.
  - **Waste Weighter** trains only on negative-outcome features (`not accurate`, `yellow card`, `red card`, `second yellow card`, `dangerous ball lost`, `missed ball`, `Foul`, `Clearance`) — 26 features.
  - Chain scores use disjoint subsets by design: `positive_chain` for `chainScore`, `harmful_chain` for `chainWasteScore`.
  - Verified: no feature appears in both subsets. Pre-implementation correlation between `playerankScore` and `wasteScore` was −0.509 (weight-vector r = −0.366). Re-run the pipeline to measure the post-implementation improvement.

- ⬜ **Asymmetric weighting**: Some action types matter more for waste than for performance (e.g. defensive errors). Allow the waste model to up-weight defensive event types relative to the performance model.

- ✅ **Harmful chain score (`chainWasteScore`)**: Implemented in full.

  **Implementation status:**

  1. ✅ **Two types of harmful chain** — `chainFeatures._compute_entity_features()` detects:
     - *Type 1 (Turnover chains)*: chain[i] ends without a shot and chain[i+1] (different team) ends in shot/goal → credits all players in chain[i] with `chain-turnover-precedes-shot/goal` and the last actor with `chain-turnover-final-actor`.
     - *Type 2 (Conceded chains)*: opponent's chain ends in shot/goal → credits defending team players who had defensive events (`DUEL`, `OTHERS`) within the chain's time window with `chain-conceded-shot/goal`. Falls back to no attribution (rather than team ID) when no specific defender is found.

  2. ✅ **Training** — `compute_harmful_chain_weights.py` trains `Weighter(label_type='l-wd')` on harmful chain features only (`filter_features(chains, 'harmful_chain')`). Output: `playerank/conf/harmful_chain_weights.json`.

  3. ✅ **Scoring** — `compute_lack_of_performance.py` applies `harmful_chain_weights.json` to player-level harmful chain features → `chainWasteScore`.

  4. ✅ **Net chain score** — `chainNetScore = chainScore − chainWasteScore` computed in `compute_lack_of_performance.py` and written to `dashboard_data.csv`.

  5. ✅ **Independence from existing scores** — Pre-implementation: `chainScore` ↔ `chainWasteScore` r = −0.891. Post-implementation the harmful chain Weighter trains on a disjoint feature set, so this correlation should drop substantially. Re-run the pipeline to confirm.

  6. ✅ **Dashboard updates** — Chain Analytics tab includes `chainScore` vs `chainWasteScore` scatter, harmful chain leaderboard, turnover vs conceded scatter, and chain origin pitch heatmap. Harmful chain map (pitch overlay of turnover positions coloured by zone) remains ⬜ not yet started.

- ✅ **Filter `playerId=0` events** — 226,038 events (7% of all data) in the Wyscout dataset have `playerId=0` (unattributed events). These are now excluded in `chainFeatures.py` at load time and filtered from the dashboard via `df[df["entity"] != 0]` in `load_data()`.
- ✅ **Role cluster labelling** — Cluster 0 (Central/Defensive Mid) covers the largest pitch zone (x 46–100, 1,538 cells) and is correctly the most populated cluster. Human-readable role labels added to the dashboard sidebar and `role_display()` helper added for all 9 clusters.
